## 1. Executive Summary

- **Model choice now includes different capability and specialization profiles; a capable model is still only a component of a production agent.** Current documentation covers foundation, reasoning, small, multimodal, and domain-specific models, recommends task-fit evaluation rather than always selecting the largest model, and distinguishes generation from systems that use tools to act. This supports an opening about broader capability choice and controlled delegation, not an invented model-release history or universal performance improvement. [S40](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/foundry/concepts/foundry-models-overview), [S04](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/cloud-adoption-framework/ai-agents/build-secure-process), [S01](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/cloud-adoption-framework/ai/strategy), [S02](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/cloud-adoption-framework/ai-agents/)
- **All three requested names are verified:** **Work IQ**, **Fabric IQ**, and **Foundry IQ**. Their primary emphases are workplace context and tools, business data and semantics, and reusable enterprise knowledge retrieval, respectively. These emphases are not mutually exclusive source boundaries: SharePoint, OneLake, and analytics can participate through different supported paths. [S06](https://learn.microsoft.com/microsoft-iq), [S07](https://learn.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/copilot/extensibility/work-iq/), [S15](https://learn.microsoft.com/fabric/iq/overview), [S27](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/foundry/agents/concepts/what-is-foundry-iq)
- **Do not assign one availability label to an entire IQ.** Work IQ has a documented GA API family but preview additions; Fabric IQ's workload and ontology are preview while several consumption features and the core data agent are GA. Foundry's readiness table calls Foundry IQ **Partial GA at API level, with portal access Preview**. Its stable minimal/extractive **Search REST** contract does not establish GA for the **knowledge-base-to-Foundry-Agent-Service MCP route**, whose documented procedure uses a preview API/SDK and a preview connection component. [S10](https://learn.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/copilot/extensibility/work-iq/cli), [S12](https://learn.microsoft.com/microsoft-copilot-studio/agents-experience/add-work-iq), [S26](https://learn.microsoft.com/fabric/fundamentals/whats-new), [S29](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/search/agentic-retrieval-overview), [S38](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/foundry/agents/how-to/foundry-iq-connect), [S39](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/foundry/concepts/general-availability)
- **Complementarity is supported; universal integration is not.** Microsoft says the IQ workloads can be used independently or together. Concrete integrations have their own scope, identity model, licensing, geography, and status; sharing an IQ name or MCP protocol does not erase those boundaries. [S27](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/foundry/agents/concepts/what-is-foundry-iq), [S23](https://learn.microsoft.com/fabric/iq/connectors/fabric-iq-mcp), [S24](https://learn.microsoft.com/fabric/data-science/data-agent-microsoft-copilot-studio-tool), [S32](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/search/agentic-knowledge-source-how-to-work-iq)

The requested three-IQ comparison remains valid as a scoped selection, but “Microsoft has exactly three IQs” is not presentation-safe. Web IQ is intentionally excluded from substantive research under this assignment; it is not proposed as another presentation pillar. [S06](https://learn.microsoft.com/microsoft-iq)

## 2. From Model Capability to Governed Agents

### Short, sourced account of model and capability evolution

**Broader choice, not just a bigger general-purpose model.** As documented on **2026-10-30**, Foundry's catalog spans foundation, reasoning, small language, multimodal, and domain/industry-specific models from Microsoft and other providers. Customers can compare models against real tasks and their own data. The useful evolution framing is a wider toolbox of capabilities and specializations, not that every newer model is better for every task. The catalog explicitly distinguishes Preview, Generally available, and Deprecated lifecycle states; listing a model is not blanket GA or regional-availability evidence. [S40](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/foundry/concepts/foundry-models-overview)

**Task fit and optional routing replace a one-model-for-everything assumption.** Microsoft's current architecture guidance recommends smaller optimized models for routine work such as summarization and reserving higher-capability models for justified complex reasoning. Foundry model router is a documented option for selecting among eligible models per request, according to routing settings and model subsets; a direct deployment is appropriate when every request must use the same model. Evaluate either approach against the workload baseline and reevaluate configuration changes. This is a dated capability account, not a claim that routing guarantees savings, chooses from every catalog model, or applies automatically across the four pillars. Router/model eligibility, deployment type, region, and feature status still need separate checks. [S04](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/cloud-adoption-framework/ai-agents/build-secure-process), [S41](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/foundry/openai/concepts/model-router), [S42](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/foundry/openai/concepts/model-router-how-it-works)

**From generating an answer to pursuing a bounded goal.** Microsoft distinguishes traditional AI for prediction/classification, generative AI for new content and responses, and agentic AI that combines models, instructions, and tools to complete tasks within permissions and controls. These capabilities can coexist. This supplies a conceptual progression into organizational context and production governance, not dated eras in which one approach universally replaces another. No benchmark improvement, model-release chronology, or exact current model-version recommendation is established here. [S01](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/cloud-adoption-framework/ai/strategy), [S02](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/cloud-adoption-framework/ai-agents/)

### A defensible conceptual progression

| Capability level | What changes | Customer implication |
|---|---|---|
| Model capability | Generative AI drafts, summarizes, answers, and generates code from requests and available context. | Evaluate the model against the task, rather than assuming the largest model is always best. [S01](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/cloud-adoption-framework/ai/strategy), [S04](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/cloud-adoption-framework/ai-agents/build-secure-process) |
| Grounded assistance | Retrieval supplies relevant organizational information the model does not inherently possess. | Source quality, business definitions, permissions, and freshness affect the answer. Grounding is not a guarantee of correctness. [S02](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/cloud-adoption-framework/ai-agents/), [S27](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/foundry/agents/concepts/what-is-foundry-iq) |
| Agentic work | The system chooses knowledge and tools, maintains state, and can perform multistep tasks. | Distinguish answering from acting; limit tools and require oversight appropriate to the action. [S02](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/cloud-adoption-framework/ai-agents/), [S05](https://learn.microsoft.com/security/zero-trust/sfi/least-privilege-for-ai-agents) |
| Production operation | Teams add ownership, evaluation, monitoring, policy enforcement, incident response, and lifecycle management. | Success includes reliable and authorized outcomes, not merely an impressive answer. [S04](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/cloud-adoption-framework/ai-agents/build-secure-process), [S05](https://learn.microsoft.com/security/zero-trust/sfi/least-privilege-for-ai-agents) |

This progression is an explanatory synthesis, not a dated model-release timeline or a requirement to maximize autonomy. Microsoft's adoption guidance supports choosing the least operationally burdensome solution that satisfies requirements, and avoiding unnecessary multi-agent complexity. Deterministic business rules remain appropriate where variability is unacceptable. [S01](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/cloud-adoption-framework/ai/strategy), [S03](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/cloud-adoption-framework/ai-agents/technology-solutions-plan-strategy), [S04](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/cloud-adoption-framework/ai-agents/build-secure-process)

### Four-pillar orientation, without a mandatory ladder

For the brief's four-pillar framing, the defensible orientation is **Microsoft 365 Copilot for work assistance**, **Microsoft Copilot Studio for creating business agents**, **GitHub Copilot for software-development assistance**, and **Microsoft Foundry for custom AI applications and agents**. The distinction is scenario and ownership, not a universal order of adoption. Microsoft's guidance explicitly includes ready-to-use agents, low-code builds, and custom builds, and acknowledges that organizations use multiple approaches. Detailed non-IQ capabilities and feature statuses must come from the corresponding product reports. [S01](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/cloud-adoption-framework/ai/strategy), [S03](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/cloud-adoption-framework/ai-agents/technology-solutions-plan-strategy)

The spectrum must not suggest “Copilot Studio is only no-code” or “Foundry is only code.” Microsoft's technology plan describes low/no-code and pro-code options in Foundry as well as low-code agent development in Copilot Studio. Fabric and the IQ capabilities belong in the supporting context/data discussion, not as a fifth main product pillar. The latter is the binding presentation scope, rather than a claim about Microsoft's complete product taxonomy. [S03](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/cloud-adoption-framework/ai-agents/technology-solutions-plan-strategy), [S15](https://learn.microsoft.com/fabric/iq/overview)

**Names and execution boundaries:** Use **Microsoft Copilot Cowork**, shortened to **Copilot Cowork**, within the brief's M365 pillar; some specialist sources retain the longer Microsoft 365 Copilot Cowork name. Separately, Copilot Studio's **GitHub Copilot harness** is a Studio authoring/orchestration runtime, **not the GitHub Copilot service**. Microsoft explicitly says Studio-agent customer data is not sent to or processed by the GitHub Copilot service through that harness. References to this harness in the IQ ledger do not describe the GitHub development-assistant service or transfer its controls to Studio. [S45](https://learn.microsoft.com/copilot/cowork/), [S37](https://learn.microsoft.com/purview/ai-copilot-cowork), [S44](https://learn.microsoft.com/microsoft-copilot-studio/harnesses-overview)

## 4. Relationships, Integration Boundaries, and Non-Claims

### What each adds, and what it does not replace

**Work IQ adds work-specific meaning and permitted actions, not unrestricted organizational access.** Its current overview organizes the offering as chat, context, tools, and workspaces. The API documentation explicitly requires signed-in-user context and does not support application-only authentication. Administrative enablement, consent, billing, and policy configuration still matter. Describing an “autonomous agent” must not imply that Work IQ suddenly supports an application-only identity. [S07](https://learn.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/copilot/extensibility/work-iq/), [S08](https://learn.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/copilot/extensibility/work-iq/api-overview), [S11](https://learn.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/copilot/extensibility/work-iq/enable-work-iq)

**Fabric IQ adds business meaning over data, not a second copy of every source or a guarantee of correct reasoning.** Semantic models retain trusted calculations; ontology can carry the source-owned measures' business context. Ontology's current design separates definitions/bindings from consumer query execution and makes graph execution optional. Shared definitions still require organizational curation and validation. The **old ontology experience** retires January 31, 2027; that notice is not retirement of the entire ontology capability. [S15](https://learn.microsoft.com/fabric/iq/overview), [S16](https://learn.microsoft.com/fabric/iq/ontology/overview)

**Foundry IQ adds reusable retrieval infrastructure, not an obligation to host every agent in Foundry.** Azure AI Search underpins the knowledge base, which can be called from Foundry Agent Service, Microsoft Agent Framework, or an application that supports the knowledge-base APIs. This architectural capability does not establish a GA Foundry-agent connection: the documented MCP route uses preview components. Indexed sources ingest content; remote sources query another service at retrieval time. The distinction affects freshness, identity, and data movement. [S27](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/foundry/agents/concepts/what-is-foundry-iq), [S28](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/foundry/agents/concepts/foundry-iq-faq), [S31](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/search/agentic-knowledge-source-overview), [S38](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/foundry/agents/how-to/foundry-iq-connect)

### Presentation-safe complementarity

- **Work context plus trusted numbers:** GA Power BI answering in Copilot Chat can combine permitted Power BI answers with files, chats, and emails. It does not mean every Fabric item participates automatically; data agents and ontologies require an explicitly published Microsoft 365 agent for that path. [S21](https://learn.microsoft.com/fabric/iq/connectors/microsoft-365-copilot-overview)

### Shared goals do not mean identical controls

Microsoft's conceptual architecture places identity, security, and governance across the ecosystem, including Microsoft Entra, Microsoft Purview, Microsoft Defender, and Microsoft Agent 365. Use that as cross-cutting framing, not as a claim that one license or switch applies an identical control set to every product and integration. Actual enforcement must be checked at the identity, tool, data-source, and consumer boundaries. [S01](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/cloud-adoption-framework/ai/strategy), [S05](https://learn.microsoft.com/security/zero-trust/sfi/least-privilege-for-ai-agents)

Two concrete exceptions are particularly useful for preventing misleading generalizations. First, each Fabric operations agent has its own **Microsoft Entra Agent ID** for attribution/auditing, but acts with its **creator's** delegated permissions through an on-behalf-of flow, even when another recipient approves a recommendation. Agent identity and effective authorization are distinct. Second, Fabric IQ MCP is read-only and user-delegated, while the Work IQ MCP surface includes create/update/delete actions. “An approval happened” and “it uses MCP” do not establish which identity or permissions execute an action. [S19](https://learn.microsoft.com/fabric/real-time-intelligence/operations-agent), [S23](https://learn.microsoft.com/fabric/iq/connectors/fabric-iq-mcp), [S09](https://learn.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/copilot/extensibility/work-iq/mcp/overview)

**Power BI in Copilot Chat has explicit DLP evidence.** Its Fabric IQ article says label-based DLP policies apply: when a policy prohibits Copilot processing content with a particular sensitivity label, Power BI content with that label is not used. This is evidence for that Chat feature, not proof of identical enforcement in Cowork. [S21](https://learn.microsoft.com/fabric/iq/connectors/microsoft-365-copilot-overview)

**Cowork protections are capability-specific.** Purview's Cowork matrix lists auditing, sensitivity labels, DLP, eDiscovery, and retention, while marking data classification and Compliance Manager unsupported for AI interactions. Detailed DLP guidance documents web-search restrictions for sensitive prompt text and label-based exclusion of supported file/email content; **sensitive-prompt blocking is preview and rolling out**. Direct prompt-file uploads are not content-scanned by this DLP control, and calendar invites are excluded from its labeled-file/email coverage. None establishes a universal inspection or protection guarantee. [S37](https://learn.microsoft.com/purview/ai-copilot-cowork), [S43](https://learn.microsoft.com/purview/dlp-microsoft365-copilot-location-learn-about)

**Cowork/Fabric IQ conflict remains unresolved at the plugin level:** Fabric's plugin article says DLP is not supported in Cowork, whereas the specialist Purview sources document the specific Cowork protections above. Use the specialist guidance for those named capabilities, but do not extrapolate it to Power BI query results or downstream Fabric-plugin workflows; no retrieved source resolves that scope. Do not promise or deny plugin-specific DLP enforcement. The plugin also explicitly lacks citations back to its source Power BI report/model today; check important numbers in the source report before reuse. [S22](https://learn.microsoft.com/fabric/iq/connectors/cowork-overview), [S37](https://learn.microsoft.com/purview/ai-copilot-cowork), [S43](https://learn.microsoft.com/purview/dlp-microsoft365-copilot-location-learn-about)

## 7. Starting Decisions, Costs, Limits, and Geography

### Starting-point guidance suitable for later presentation development

Start with a bounded business outcome and where users already work. Evaluate an existing experience before adding a custom agent; choose low-code or custom development according to control, integration, and operating requirements. Add the organizational context actually needed: workplace context, governed business metrics, authoritative documents, or a justified combination. This is a decision aid, not a migration ladder or a recommendation to purchase the entire ecosystem. [S01](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/cloud-adoption-framework/ai/strategy), [S03](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/cloud-adoption-framework/ai-agents/technology-solutions-plan-strategy), [S27](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/foundry/agents/concepts/what-is-foundry-iq)

Before expanding autonomy, verify the specific feature path's status and identity model, establish evaluation criteria, test unauthorized-user behavior, and define a human escalation/recovery route.
