Start where the job fits. Combine layers as needed.

Orient the audience before describing features.
Microsoft 365 Copilot is the familiar work experience.
Copilot Studio is an option for tailoring business agents.
Foundry provides more control over custom AI applications and agent implementations.
GitHub Copilot supports the people building and maintaining software, including software that may use Studio or Foundry.
Its app also supports repository-optional Projects and canvases for planning, feedback summaries, and artifacts.
That broader productivity role is not the M365 app runtime or its permission model.

The spectrum describes emphasis, not exclusive capabilities.
Studio is not limited to business users, and Foundry is not code-only.
There is customization within the M365 experience too.
The left-to-right spectrum shows typical authoring/control emphasis, not a
universal capability ranking or a required path through the products.
GitHub Copilot can help work across these layers rather than being a compulsory
runtime stage between Studio and Foundry.

An organization may use several layers, but a particular task may need only one.
Appearing together here does not establish a direct integration between every pair or shared permissions across them.
For example, a work briefing may begin in M365, a process-specific agent in Studio, and a tailored customer application in Foundry.
We will make the combinations concrete after introducing the context layer.
