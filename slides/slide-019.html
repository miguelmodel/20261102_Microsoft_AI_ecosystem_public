Data grounding

Supply relevant organizational information and business context to the model.

  • Use relevant knowledge.
  • Keep information current for the job.
  • Retrieve only authorized information.

Grounding supplies information the model may not know from training.
Retrieval-augmented generation is one pattern: find relevant content and include it in the input.
Source quality, shared business definitions, freshness, and permissions affect the answer.
Grounding grants no new access and does not guarantee correctness.
Retrieved content can also contain misleading or hostile instructions, so treat it as untrusted input and test the complete result.
The later IQ section distinguishes specific context capabilities.
Next, consider the dependable environment around them.
