The right model for the right task

Foundry brings a broad catalog of model families together for discovery,
comparison and deployment.
These are examples, not an exhaustive catalog or a ranking.
Compare quality, latency, cost and control using representative work and data.
Model choice remains a task-fit decision as the catalog evolves.
