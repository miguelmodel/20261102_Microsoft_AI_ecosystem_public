Imagine an account manager preparing for a customer review.
Recent work discussions explain the relationship; a permitted Power BI report supplies the relevant business numbers and definitions.
Copilot Chat can combine these inputs into a briefing for review.
This is an illustrative scenario, not a measured customer outcome.

The scope is Power BI reports and semantic models, not every Fabric artifact.
The business numbers are only as current as the model's latest successful refresh.
The required Copilot Premium license and licensed, permissioned Power BI access still apply.
Power BI answering in Copilot Chat is GA in the approved documentation, with report/model scope and refresh-dependent data.

Check the briefing against its sources before sharing.
That combines useful context and trusted business meaning without removing human responsibility.

Optional detail; skip if short on time:
Chat's Power BI feature applies label-based data loss prevention restrictions.
That does not establish identical protection for the separate Cowork plugin; its result-level protection remains unresolved in Microsoft's documentation.
