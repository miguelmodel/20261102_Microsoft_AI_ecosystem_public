These are the three IQs covered here, selected from Microsoft's wider IQ family.
They are supporting context capabilities, not replacements for the four product layers.

Work IQ supplies workplace context and appropriate tools.
Fabric IQ supplies business meaning and trusted metrics.
Foundry IQ supplies reusable retrieval of relevant knowledge.

These are primary jobs, not exclusive file-format or structured/unstructured partitions.
Sources can overlap through supported routes.
Independent use and specific combinations are documented, not universal knowledge-source interchangeability.

For an illustrative briefing, recent work discussions, a business measure and an approved policy answer different questions.
That is an information-needs example, not an automatic three-IQ integration.
