Recovery

Infrastructure includes hosting, storage, networks, and dependent services, not just a model endpoint.
Test capacity across retrieval, tools, and application dependencies.
Use deliberate timeouts, appropriate retries, and graceful degradation rather than hiding errors.
Preserve state where needed and test interrupted work.
Managed hosting reduces burden but does not remove end-to-end customer design and integration responsibilities.
No unlimited capacity, universal private-network claim, or cross-product SLA is implied.
Next, coordinate the work that runs on that infrastructure.
