How long can an agent work on its own?

Length of software tasks (timed by human experts) that AI agents complete successfully 50% of the time.

Source: METR, Task-Completion Time Horizons (TH1.1). Published historical series.

This is a historical benchmark of evaluated systems on software-oriented tasks.
The horizon describes human-expert task difficulty at a fitted 50% success threshold, not how long an agent runs unattended.
The tested system includes a model, scaffold, tools, and evaluation conditions.
It is not a guarantee for customer business tasks.
Dates are METR-recorded model-release fields, not general-availability dates.
