Microsoft AI Ecosystem

Open with the customer's practical question: “Where should we start, and what should we combine?”
The purpose is not to select one winner or prescribe a journey through every product.
It is to understand where ready-to-use assistance fits, when a business agent needs tailoring, and where software development or custom application control matters.

Our four layers are Microsoft 365 Copilot, including Copilot Cowork; Copilot Studio; GitHub Copilot; and Microsoft Foundry.
We will also distinguish the three IQs covered here, which supply different kinds of organizational context rather than three additional product pillars.

Set the central expectation: a model's capability alone does not establish a useful, authorized production outcome.
We also need relevant information, clear ownership, and appropriate controls.
Choose for the job, add the right context, and keep control.
First follow the supplied evolution and transportation opening, then locate the products.
